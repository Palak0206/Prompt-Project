## Scheduling Decisions

- **Earliest deadline first:** Mathematics is the first exam, hard, and only 40% done, so it gets most of the time (about 2.5-3 hrs/day) from 16-19 Nov.
- **DBMS in parallel:** It is medium difficulty and 60% done, so about 1-1.5 hrs/day is enough to finish the syllabus, and it gets a heavier revision push on 20-21 Nov.
- **OS starts later:** It is hard and only 30% done, but it has the latest exam. It gets a gentle start on 20 Nov, then dominates the schedule from 22 Nov onward, about 17 hours in total.
- **Revision built in:** Each subject gets a full revision session and a timed previous-year paper in the 1-2 days before its exam (19 Nov for Maths, 21 Nov for DBMS, 24 Nov for OS).
- **Exam days:** These are kept light (30 minutes to 1 hour of revision) so you stay fresh. Remaining time goes to the next subject.

**Tip:** Take a 10-minute break after every 90 minutes, and sleep at least 7 hours, especially before exams. If you fall behind on any day, borrow time from the DBMS slots and keep Maths and OS problem-solving sessions intact.
