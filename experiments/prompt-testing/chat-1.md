## **3. Scheduling decisions**

1\. **Mathematics (16–20 November):** Gets the highest priority initially because it is difficult, only 40% prepared, and has the earliest examination. The last day focuses on revision and mock testing rather than learning entirely new concepts.

2\. **DBMS (16–22 November):** Receives shorter sessions initially to maintain preparation. After the Mathematics examination, its study hours increase significantly.

3\. **Operating Systems (16–25 November):** Receives consistent daily attention because it is difficult and only 30% complete. After the DBMS examination, it becomes the sole preparation priority.

4\. **Revision strategy:** Mock tests, previous-year questions (PYQs), formula revision and correction of mistakes are included closer to each examination.

## **4. Important study rules**

- Take a 5–10 minute break between study sessions.
- Prioritize understanding concepts before memorizing answers.
- Maintain a separate notebook for formulas, definitions and difficult questions.
- Don't spend the entire revision period rereading notes; actively solve questions.
- Sleep properly before each examination.

**Most important:** Complete the syllabus first, practice questions second, and revise strategically before each exam. Don't aim for perfection in every topic at the cost of leaving entire chapters untouched.
